# 0004: Close Discover on P1 and desk evidence; move P2's sessions to Validate

- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Department:** UX research
- **Phase:** Discover
- **Decided by:** Arno

## Context

The research plan included an observed sign-up and an interview with P2. Booking them would delay Discover, and the round has a 3-week appetite (live by 25 Oct 2026).

## Options considered

1. **Wait for P2's sessions before closing Discover.** Pros: two users' evidence. Cons: delays Explore by an unknown amount.
2. **Close Discover on P1's baseline and the desk research, and move P2's sessions to Validate.** Pros: keeps the date; P2's first sign-up becomes a clean first-time test of the *redesigned* onboarding. Cons: every user insight rests on one runner, who is also the designer.

## Decision

Option 2. Discover closes with P1's baseline, P1's sync check and the desk research. P2's sign-up and interview happen in Validate, on the redesigned screens.

## Evidence

- P1 baseline and sync check (`docs/research/log.md`).
- Desk research (`docs/research/desk/today-and-explanations.md`).

## Consequences

- Easier: Explore starts now.
- Harder: Arno-as-user bias (brief risk 2) is at its maximum. Insights are reported as "1 of 1" and treated as hypotheses until P2 confirms or contradicts them.
- No onboarding baseline exists, so the case study can't show before/after numbers for onboarding, only the redesigned result.
- Revisit: if P2's Validate sessions contradict an insight, go back to Define for that insight.

## Case-study note

I moved the second runner's research into validation, which kept the timeline and turned their first sign-up into a clean test of the new design.
