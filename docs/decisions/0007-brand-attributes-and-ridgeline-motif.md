# 0007: Brand attributes Rugged, Grounded, Relentless, with a ridgeline motif built from our own data

- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Department:** Visual design (brand)
- **Phase:** Design
- **Decided by:** Arno

## Context

The visual layer needs attributes that every type and colour choice traces back to (visual-design skill). Arno chose the name Khoisān for the San persistence-hunting heritage: running antelope down over hours, in heat, over rough ground (documented by Liebenberg, *Current Anthropology*, 2006). Arno's brief words: terrain, rough nature (sand, forest, beach, mountain), "manly", a cultural symbol, a mountain-outline loading screen and a mountain-shaped progress bar. The reference was the MUT website's stacked-ridgeline course profile.

## Options considered

1. **Use a San cultural symbol** (rock-art figure, eland). *Rejected for round 1:* the South African San Council's Code of Ethics (2017) asks for consent and benefit-sharing before San culture is used. The route, if pursued, is to contact the San Council.
2. **Recreate the MUT graphic's look.** *Rejected:* it's UTMB's brand asset; a look-alike would read as an official product.
3. **Our own motif from the values and the terrain.** Chosen.

## Decision

- **Attributes:**
  - **Rugged:** strong, slightly condensed type, raw texture, no gloss.
  - **Grounded:** an earthy palette (sand, fynbos, rock, ocean) over strong neutrals.
  - **Relentless:** steady rhythm, a continuous-line motif, progress that accumulates.
- **Inclusive:** "manly" is dropped; the app should feel tough to anyone who runs it.
- **Motif:** our own ridgeline drawn from real MUT 60 elevation data. It's used for the loading screen (static under reduced motion) and for season progress drawn as the course profile (base → build → peak → taper → race, "week n of N"), which uses data the app already has.
- The persistence-hunting story appears in the case study as *what inspired me*, not as a claim about a people.

## Evidence

- Visual-design skill: brand = positioning + voice + visual identity; every choice traceable to attributes.
- South African San Council, San Code of Research Ethics (2017).
- Liebenberg (2006), "Persistence hunting by modern hunter-gatherers", *Current Anthropology*.

## Consequences

- Needs the real MUT 60 elevation data (GPX) before the motif ships; the moodboard uses an illustrative ridgeline until then.
- The progress motif adds a visual element to Today's header, using existing data only (no engine change).

## Case-study note

I took the inspiration from a story of endurance and drew the identity from the terrain and our own data, not from borrowed symbols.
