# 0002: Claude makes the design and code; Arno directs as Design Lead

- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Department:** Product strategy
- **Phase:** Brief
- **Decided by:** Arno

## Context

In the portfolio project, Claude acts as mentor and Arno does the hands-on work to build skill. Khoisān has a hard clock that the portfolio doesn't: the training season for MUT 60 (29 May 2027) is already running, and the first redesign needs to be live by 25 Oct 2026. Arno doesn't have the hours to learn Figma craft and ship Khoisān at the same time.

## Options considered

1. **Arno makes, Claude mentors** (the portfolio model). Pros: maximum skill growth. Cons: far slower; most of the season would be trained on the baseline.
2. **Claude makes, Arno directs.** Pros: fast, keeps the standards and records. Cons: the craft in the frames isn't Arno's own, which the case study has to say.
3. **Skip the design process and patch the app.** Pros: fastest. Cons: no case study, and no evidence the changes help.

## Decision

In Khoisān, Claude produces the Figma work and the code. Arno is the Design Lead who frames the problem, runs the research with Daan, chooses between directions, and approves every gate. Arno builds hands-on Figma skill in the portfolio project instead.

## Evidence

- Arno's time constraints and the season deadline (brief, Appetite).
- Portfolio decision 0001 positions Arno as a Product Designer with AI-assisted design-to-code as the differentiator, so directing an AI maker is consistent with that story (*judgement*).

## Consequences

- Easier: Khoisān can ship in weeks, with every decision still recorded.
- Harder: the case study must be specific about who did what. It is labelled **AI-assisted**, and its AI section states that Claude made the frames and code from Arno's direction. In interviews, Arno answers "did you build these frames?" with the same split.
- Arno must be able to explain every design decision; the decision records are the evidence.
- Revisit if Arno's Figma skill from the portfolio project reaches the point where Arno can take over the making.

## Case-study note

I led this project as Design Lead with an AI maker, which let us ship inside a training season while keeping every decision traceable.
